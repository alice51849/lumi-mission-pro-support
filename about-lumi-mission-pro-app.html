1. What it is

Lumi Mission Planet Pro is the paid-download edition of a reward and routine app for young children: five daily routines become gentle missions, and the buddy the child picked cheers on every tap.

  • Five daily missions: meals, tooth brushing, sleep time, tidy-up and school prep
  • Unlimited custom missions with your own icon and scene for each
  • Six buddy characters and one hundred achievement stickers
  • Parent dashboard: today at a glance, seven-day calendar and a tap-by-tap history
  • CSV export, one-tap backup, an optional daily reminder and 44 languages

2. Who it is for

Parents of children aged about three to seven, to be used together. It suits daily moments such as getting ready for school, brushing teeth, mealtime, tidy-up and bedtime. No advertising, no account; everything stays on the device.

3. How it is paid for

This is a paid download: one purchase unlocks everything, and it supports Family Sharing. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads.
